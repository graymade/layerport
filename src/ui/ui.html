Layerport

Figma to layered PSD
Select frames or whole Sections, then export. Text stays live and editable, image layers embed as hi-res Smart Objects (up to 3x, capped at 6000px edge). Groups, blend modes, and opacity carry over. Files land in your Downloads folder.
Batch rename map (optional)
Frames tagged [#ID] in their name export under the filename mapped to that ID. Paste JSON ({"123": "filename"}, [{"id", "filename"}] rows) or CSV lines (id,filename).
Open source (MIT). Runs entirely in Figma, no network access. Made by Prisma.
